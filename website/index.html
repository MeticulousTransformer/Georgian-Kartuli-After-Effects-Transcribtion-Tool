Before you start

Do I need an AI subscription?

You can explore the demo and import word-timed JSON without a provider account. Cloud transcription needs an API account and may charge for audio. Start with ElevenLabs for Georgian and review the result on your own recording. Local Whisper options need extra setup.

Will this work on my trimmed footage?

Transcription currently starts at the source file’s beginning. Selecting a moved, trimmed or retimed layer does not apply that change to the transcript. Align the generated precomp with your footage and check playback.

What stays on my computer?

Provider keys, edited transcript exports and project files stay local. Cloud transcription sends extracted audio to the provider you choose. Local providers can work offline after their models are installed. This website has no account system or analytics.

How do updates work?

Inside CapForge, open Setup and choose Check for updates. The app verifies the download for your OS and opens Setup. Save your AE project, close AE, and finish installation. Mac and Windows are published together from the same code.

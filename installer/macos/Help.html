CAPFORGE / MAC

Your words. In motion.

  1. Drag CapForge from the disk image to Applications, then open it there.
  2. Close After Effects. For this unsigned preview, select Allow unsigned Adobe extensions for this account. This allows all unsigned Adobe panels for your account.
  3. Click Install CapForge. First setup downloads the private runtime and transcription packages; keep your internet connection available.
  4. Restart After Effects. Open Window → Extensions → CapForge.
  5. Open the panel’s Setup section to add your AI account. Choose your source, language, and caption style.

Updates and repair

Open the CapForge app and choose Update / Repair. If the installed runtime is newer than your app, Repair uses the installed version to avoid downgrading it. Download the latest Mac app from the official release before updating. Your keys, exports, and saved looks stay in your per-user runtime. The local service starts in the background after setup.

Where your files live

The app lives in Applications. Its runtime lives at ~/Library/Application Support/CapForge. An existing owned Kartuli Caption Forge runtime keeps its original location. The panel lives in your Adobe CEP extensions folder. Use Logs after setup to find ~/Library/Logs/CapForge/setup.log.

If macOS blocks this preview

Check the release notes for your download’s signing status. Older previews are not Apple notarized. After trying to open a preview you trust, macOS may offer Open Anyway in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Organization policies can prevent opening unnotarized apps. An approved app can still show a normal first-open confirmation.

If the panel does not appear

Close After Effects, reopen CapForge, enable the unsigned preview option, and run Repair. Then restart After Effects. Review the setup log if installation fails.

Georgian and English speech is processed by the provider you select. Provider keys stay in the local backend configuration. Normalized word timing and editable caption layers remain the common foundation for every design.
